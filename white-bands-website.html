The chambers

A family practice, run the way chambers should be.

White Bands is a family-founded practice at Mira Road East, led by three principal advocates and supported by a team of twenty. That shape is deliberate. The principals sit together and read a file before the first hearing, keep a single account of where every matter stands, and make sure a client is spoken to by the person who will actually appear.

Most of our work comes from people who have received a notice, a summons, or an order they did not expect — a suit over inherited land, an FIR, a cheque that has bounced, a maintenance or custody dispute, a claim rejected by an insurer. The first meeting is for facts, not for fees: what the documents say, which court has jurisdiction, what the limitation period is, and what an honest range of outcomes looks like.

We draft our own pleadings and argue our own matters, and the size of the team means a matter keeps moving on the days its principal is in another court. Where a case genuinely calls for senior counsel or an expert — a handwriting examiner, a valuer, a revenue record search — we say so early rather than late.

Practice

Four areas, and the work each of them involves.

Civil & property

Suits and defences concerning land, houses and money. Partition between co-sharers, possession and ejectment, specific performance of agreements to sell, injunctions against dispossession, and correction of revenue records where a mutation has gone wrong.

  • Title & partition suits
  • Specific performance
  • Injunctions
  • Landlord–tenant
  • Mutation & records
  • RERA & builder disputes

Criminal

Representation from the first information through to trial and appeal. Bail and anticipatory bail, complaints and applications for quashing, trials before magistrates and sessions courts, and prosecution and defence of cheque dishonour complaints.

  • Bail & anticipatory bail
  • Sessions trials
  • Quashing petitions
  • Private complaints
  • Cheque dishonour, s.138 NI Act
  • Appeals & revisions

Family & matrimonial

Divorce and judicial separation, restitution and nullity; maintenance for wives, children and parents; custody and guardianship; and succession — probate, letters of administration and partition of ancestral property. Protection and residence orders in domestic violence proceedings.

  • Divorce & separation
  • Maintenance
  • Custody & guardianship
  • Succession & probate
  • Domestic violence
  • Mediation & settlement

Consumer, writ & service

Complaints before the District and State Consumer Commissions — insurance, builder delay, defective goods and deficient medical or banking service. Writ petitions in the High Court, and service matters arising from appointment, promotion, termination and pension.

  • Consumer complaints
  • Insurance claims
  • Builder delay
  • Writ petitions
  • Service & pension
  • Co-operative society matters
